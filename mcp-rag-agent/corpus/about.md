# About Nexora Technologies

Nexora Technologies is a fictional enterprise software company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company specializes in building intelligent automation platforms for mid-market and enterprise customers.

## Company Overview

Nexora was founded by Dr. Elena Vasquez and Marcus Chen after they left their positions at a major cloud provider. Their goal was to create a unified platform that combines workflow automation, data integration, and AI-driven decision-making into a single product suite.

The company employs approximately 450 people across three offices: Austin (headquarters), Toronto (engineering), and London (sales and customer success). Annual recurring revenue reached $38 million in fiscal year 2024, up from $22 million in FY2023.

## Mission and Vision

**Mission:** To eliminate repetitive operational work so teams can focus on high-value decisions.

**Vision:** A world where every business process that can be automated is automated, and the ones that cannot are augmented by intelligent assistants.

## Core Values

1. **Customer Obsession** - Every product decision starts with a customer pain point.
2. **Radical Transparency** - Internal metrics, financials, and decision rationale are shared company-wide.
3. **Ship and Iterate** - Launch early, measure outcomes, and improve continuously.
4. **Own the Outcome** - Individuals and teams are accountable for results, not just activities.

## Leadership Team

- **Dr. Elena Vasquez** - Co-founder and CEO. Former VP of Engineering at a major cloud provider. PhD in Distributed Systems from MIT.
- **Marcus Chen** - Co-founder and CTO. Previously led the automation team at a Fortune 100 tech company. MS in Computer Science from Stanford.
- **Priya Nair** - Chief Revenue Officer. 15 years of enterprise SaaS sales experience. Joined Nexora in 2021.
- **David Okonkwo** - VP of Engineering. Formerly at a Y Combinator startup. Leads the Toronto engineering office.
- **Sarah Kim** - VP of Product. Joined from a leading workflow automation vendor in 2022.

## Funding History

- **Seed Round (2019):** $3.5 million from angel investors and a local accelerator.
- **Series A (2020):** $12 million led by Horizon Ventures.
- **Series B (2022):** $45 million led by Summit Capital, with participation from Horizon Ventures.
- **Series C (2024):** $80 million led by Apex Growth Partners. Post-money valuation of $520 million.

Total funding raised to date: $140.5 million.
