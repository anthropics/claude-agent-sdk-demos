## Case Studies

### BrightPath Financial - Loan Processing Automation

**Industry:** Financial Services
**Size:** 2,400 employees
**Products Used:** Nexora Flow, Nexora Connect

**Challenge:** BrightPath's loan approval process required data from 7 different internal systems and 3 external credit bureaus. Loan officers spent 45 minutes per application manually pulling and verifying data.

**Solution:** Using Nexora Flow, BrightPath automated the entire data-gathering phase of loan processing. Nexora Connect synchronized applicant data across all internal systems in real time. A workflow triggers automatically when a new application is submitted, pulls data from all sources, runs preliminary validation checks, and presents a unified view to the loan officer.

**Results:**
- Reduced per-application processing time from 45 minutes to 8 minutes.
- Decreased data entry errors by 92%.
- Improved loan approval turnaround from 5 business days to 1.5 business days.
- Annual cost savings of approximately $1.2 million.
- 100% of loan applications now processed through the automated pipeline.

### MediTrack Health Systems - Patient Data Integration

**Industry:** Healthcare
**Size:** 5,100 employees
**Products Used:** Nexora Connect, Nexora Assist

**Challenge:** MediTrack operated 12 clinics across three states, each using different electronic health record (EHR) systems. Patient data was siloed, making it difficult to provide coordinated care when patients visited multiple locations.

**Solution:** Nexora Connect established real-time data pipelines between all 12 EHR systems and a central data warehouse. PII detection and field-level encryption ensured HIPAA compliance. Nexora Assist was deployed to help clinical staff query patient histories using natural language.

**Results:**
- Achieved a unified patient view across all 12 clinics within 6 months.
- Reduced duplicate diagnostic tests by 34% due to better data visibility.
- Saved an estimated $2.8 million annually in redundant testing and administrative overhead.
- Clinical staff adoption of the natural-language query interface reached 78% within 3 months.
- Zero compliance incidents related to data synchronization since deployment.

### QuickCart - E-commerce Order Fulfillment

**Industry:** E-commerce
**Size:** 380 employees
**Products Used:** Nexora Flow, Nexora Connect, Nexora Assist

**Challenge:** QuickCart experienced rapid growth from 5,000 orders/day to 25,000 orders/day over 18 months. Their manual order routing process could not keep up, leading to fulfillment delays and inventory mismatches.

**Solution:** Nexora Flow automated the entire order routing pipeline, including inventory checks, warehouse selection based on proximity and stock levels, shipping carrier selection, and customer notification. Nexora Connect integrated their Shopify storefront, three warehouse management systems, and four shipping carriers. Nexora Assist monitored the pipeline and flagged anomalies.

**Results:**
- Order processing time reduced from 4 hours to 12 minutes average.
- Fulfillment error rate dropped from 3.8% to 0.4%.
- On-time delivery rate improved from 87% to 96%.
- Handled the 5x order volume increase with no additional operations headcount.
- Nexora Assist detected and alerted on 23 potential stockout situations in Q4 2024, preventing an estimated $340,000 in lost sales.

### TerraForm Construction - Project Management Automation

**Industry:** Construction
**Size:** 1,800 employees
**Products Used:** Nexora Flow

**Challenge:** TerraForm managed 40+ active construction projects simultaneously. Project status updates, budget tracking, and resource allocation were managed through a combination of spreadsheets, email, and a legacy project management tool. Project managers spent 10+ hours per week on status reporting alone.

**Solution:** Nexora Flow automated status report generation by pulling data from the project management tool, accounting system, and time-tracking application. Weekly reports are generated automatically and distributed to stakeholders. Budget threshold alerts trigger notifications when spending approaches limits.

**Results:**
- Reduced project manager reporting time from 10 hours/week to 1 hour/week.
- Budget overrun detection improved from monthly review cycles to real-time alerts.
- Three potential budget overruns (totaling $780,000) were caught and addressed early in Q3 2024.
- Overall project delivery timeline improved by 11% across the portfolio.
